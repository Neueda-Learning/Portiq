# Summary

Portiq uses Generative AI to tackle three practical problems for someone tracking their own portfolio.

Getting data in is a pain. Typing holdings in by hand from a broker export is slow and error-prone, so Smart File Import and Statement Scanning use text and vision models to turn whatever format a broker throws at you, or even a photo of a paper statement, into clean, structured holdings data.

Raw numbers aren't always meaningful. Metrics like beta, Sharpe ratio, or 52-week price position make sense to an experienced investor but can be pretty opaque to everyone else, so the Insights Summary and Recommendation Narration turn those numbers into plain-English explanations that don't require a finance background.

And algorithmic decisions need explaining, not just stating. The recommendation engine produces its calls from a scoring algorithm, and AI narration keeps that decision exactly as the algorithm made it while presenting the reasoning in language a person can actually use.

In every case the AI is doing what it's actually good at: understanding language, cleaning up messy formats, writing natural sentences, while the math, the financial logic, and the real decisions stay in deterministic, testable code.
